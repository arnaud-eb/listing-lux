# Inter-Rater Sample — Blind Scoring

**Read first:** `docs/audits/2026-04-llm-output-audit/appendix-a-rubric.md`. The rubric defines all 10 dimensions and their anchors.

**Your task:** for each of the 6 generated listings below, score 9 dimensions on a 1-5 integer scale. **Do NOT look at the LLM judge's scores yet** — those are in `docs/audits/2026-04-llm-output-audit/runs/1.3/<id>-<lang>.score.json` and you should treat them as confidential until you're done. `cross_lang_consistency` is not scored here (it requires seeing all 4 languages of a fixture; not in scope for the inter-rater).

**How agreement is computed:** for each dimension d, agreement(d) = count(your.score == judge.score ± 1) / 6. If agreement < 0.70 for any dimension, that dimension's findings in the audit will be flagged as low-confidence.

**Time budget:** ~30 minutes. Edit the score blocks below directly with your numbers + a one-line justification.
